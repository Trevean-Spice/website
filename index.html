Good Questions

Asked and answered

How does the NFC tap actually work?

Every lid carries an NFC tag — the same technology as tap-to-pay. Hold your phone near the lid and your jar's own page opens: farm, grower, harvest date, and live freshness for that exact batch. No app, no account.

Why does freshness matter so much?

Ground spices lose their volatile aromatic oils from the day they're processed. Most grocery jars sit in supply chains for one to three years before purchase. Trevean jars are batch-dated and sealed within weeks of harvest.

What does the subscription cost?

Spice Sage is $39/month or $99/quarter — the five signature blends, rotating limited releases, and recipe pairings unlocked by tapping the jar. Individual jars are $18.

Where do the spices come from?

Direct relationships with 25+ artisan growers across five global regions — Sichuan peppercorn, Uji green tea, Kashan saffron and rose, High Atlas herbs, Saint Ann allspice. Every grower is named on the jar.
