# Conversion Optimization — Recommendations
*Ranked by expected impact for a pre-launch DTC subscription brand.*

## Shipped this round
- Working cart with persistent state and a no-dead-end checkout fallback (waitlist → email)
- Subscription presented as the hero offer ($39/$99 tiers, "Best value" anchor on quarterly)
- "Join the Spice Ledger" email capture with first-batch-access incentive
- Real social proof (92% retention, NPS 82, 200-member beta) instead of invented logos
- FAQ section answering the four highest-intent objections (how NFC works, why freshness, price, sourcing)

## Do next (high impact)
1. **Real grower photography.** The grower profile is the emotional close of every blend page and it's currently a placeholder rectangle. This is the single highest-leverage asset purchase on the site.
2. **Per-blend jar renders.** Every jar render is the Persian Sunrise mockup; product cards showing the wrong label quietly erode the "transparency" promise.
3. **Connect the email endpoint** (Sender.net is already on the AIOS pending list). Pre-launch, the email list *is* the conversion — make the hero's primary CTA "Get first-batch access" until checkout is live.
4. **Exit-intent or scroll-75% Ledger prompt** — one per session, dismissible. Pre-launch sites convert 2–4× more email with a second placement.
5. **Compiled Tailwind + WebP images.** Sub-2s LCP on mobile is a conversion feature, not an engineering nicety. The CDN compiler and 10MB PNGs are the two blockers to Lighthouse 95+.
